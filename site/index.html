Booking in three steps on iPhone

  1. Pick seats on the cinema’s own map

    Customers pinch and zoom the auditorium. Each seat is held on your server with a countdown, so two people never buy the same one.

  2. Add food and drink to the booking

    Sizes, combos and stock come from your menu. Customers collect at the counter, or have it brought to their seat where you offer that.

  3. Open the ticket in the app

    The QR ticket is stored on the phone, so it opens with no signal. Totals and fees always come from your server.

Questions

Straight answers about what works today and what comes next.

Email us a question

Is it a native app or a web view?

Native. The app is built in SwiftUI for iPhone, and the seat map is drawn on the phone.

Do we get our own App Store listing?

Yes. Each operator gets its own build with its own name and icon. Everything else changes from the portal.

Does the app connect to our ticketing system?

Not yet. The connection layer is ready, and each adapter is written once we have licensed access to that system.

How does the app take payments?

Checkout runs in a test mode today. Apple Pay arrives through a payment provider once your merchant account is set up.
