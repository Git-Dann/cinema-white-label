Questions

Straight answers about what works today, what comes next and what stays the same.

Email us a question

Is it a native app or a web view?

Native. The app is built in SwiftUI for iPhone, and the seat map is drawn on the phone.

Do we get our own App Store listing?

Yes. Each operator gets its own build with its own name and icon. Everything else changes from the portal.

Is it only for big chains?

No. A single-screen independent gets the same app, portal and seat maps as a national chain. Chains also get head office planning and roles for every site.

Where does the forecast come from?

The year’s release calendar, your past trading and the school holidays. The demo runs on sample data; with your account it runs on your own bookings.

Can we run more than one cinema?

Yes. Each venue has its own screens, seat maps, menus and features, all under one operator account.

Can customers order food to their seat?

Yes, at venues that turn on seat delivery. Food goes into the same booking as the tickets, priced by the server.

Who owns the booking data?

You do. Every step from first tap to payment is yours: films viewed, seats picked, food added and drop-outs. Personal details are stripped, and customers can opt out.

Do you support loyalty and memberships?

Not in the app yet. Points, memberships and gift cards are already modelled on the server and come next.

Do we have to replace our ticketing system?

No. Seatfold is your customer app and portal. It connects through an adapter, written once we have licensed access to your system.

How does the app take payments?

Checkout runs in a test mode today. Apple Pay arrives through a payment provider once your merchant account is set up.
